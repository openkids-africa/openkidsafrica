We visited H&C Nursery and primary school at Ibadan for an interactive session with the **kids**.

The children were excited and happy to learn about technology for health, sport, and games. We showed them how to leverage resources from the&nbsp;[#TechSheCan](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=techshecan&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6875424814144872448)&nbsp;Project.

It was interesting to learn about their future career choices; we showed them how Information Technology cuts across every field of interest. We learned about their limitations, which are not limited to the children but the school – the lack of internet and digital devices. It was an exciting moment for us at Pranet Technology Initiative, and we look forward to bringing technology awareness to kids in other schools in rural communities in Nigeria.

Thanks to Tech She Can for creating a platform where children worldwide can learn about technology and gain digital skills for future jobs.
